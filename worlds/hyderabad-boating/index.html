Assets: Poly Haven (CC0) textures and HDRI skies, three.js water normals (MIT). Terrain, boats, landmarks, vegetation and all audio are procedural. Landmarks are modelled from memory and proportion, not survey data.

How to boat

Open the throttle, steer with the rudder, and watch the depth under the keel — run onto a sandbar and you will stop.

What you are looking at

Hussain Sagar — the tank Ibrahim Quli Qutb Shah's son-in-law dug in 1563; the 18 m Buddha went up on Gibraltar Rock in 1992. Durgam Cheruvu — granite among the oldest rock on earth, with the 2020 cable bridge over it. The Musi — Purana Pul (1578) and the Charminar (1591) above the south bank. The village tank — the irrigation pond that every Telangana village keeps.
